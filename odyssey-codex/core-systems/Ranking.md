# Ranking System

## Definition

The Ranking System measures a member's accumulated long-term growth.

Unlike Performance, Ranking represents trust, maturity and proven consistency over time.

Ranking is a permanent record of growth rather than a reflection of current performance.

---

# Purpose

The Ranking System exists to:

- Recognize long-term growth.
- Reward consistency.
- Unlock future opportunities.
- Build meaningful career progression.

Performance represents today.

Ranking represents the journey.

---

# Why

Many organizations promote employees based only on short-term performance.

Odyssey believes promotion should represent long-term trust rather than temporary success.

Members earn Rank by consistently demonstrating:

- Professional capability
- Responsibility
- Positive influence
- Long-term reliability

Ranking represents the Guild's trust in a member's ability to take on greater responsibility.

---

# What is NOT

Ranking is NOT:

- Monthly Performance
- Salary Grade
- Job Position
- Popularity
- Seniority

Ranking represents accumulated growth.

# Current Principles

Ranking is earned.

Ranking is permanent.

Ranking normally does not decrease.

Temporary poor performance should not erase years of growth.

Ranking is not awarded for performance alone.

It represents the Guild's long-term confidence in a member.

Members must demonstrate capability, consistency, responsibility, and positive influence before advancing.

# Design History

Originally,

Ranking was considered a higher level of Performance.

Later,

Odyssey separated the two concepts.

Performance became today's contribution.

Ranking became lifetime growth.

This separation became one of Odyssey's core philosophies.
