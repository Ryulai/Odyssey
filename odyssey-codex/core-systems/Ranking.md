# Ranking System

## Definition

The Ranking System measures a member's accumulated long-term growth.

Unlike Performance, Ranking represents trust, maturity and proven consistency over time.

Ranking is a permanent record of growth rather than a reflection of current performance.

---

# Purpose

The Ranking System exists to:

- Recognize long-term growth.
- Reward consistency.
- Unlock future opportunities.
- Build meaningful career progression.

Performance represents today.

Ranking represents the journey.

---

# Why

Many organizations reward only short-term results.

Odyssey believes true growth is built through continuous improvement over time.

Ranking encourages members to become better people, not only better performers.

---

# What is NOT

Ranking is NOT:

- Monthly Performance
- Salary Grade
- Job Position
- Popularity
- Seniority

Ranking represents accumulated growth.

# Rank Philosophy

## Apprentice

Learning.

Observation.

Building basic habits.

---

## Bronze

"I Can Do It."

Able to complete personal responsibilities independently.

---

## Silver

"You Can Trust Me."

Consistent, reliable and dependable.

---

## Gold

"I Can Influence."

Qualified to unlock the Secondary Class System.

Trusted to expand beyond one profession.

---

## Platinum

Professional excellence.

---

## Diamond

Exceptional consistency.

High organizational value.

---

## Master

Recognized expert.

Able to influence standards.

---

## Mystical

Cross-domain influence.

Represents rare professional maturity.

---

## Legend

The highest recognition inside Odyssey.

Represents legacy rather than status.

# Current Principles

Ranking is earned.

Ranking is permanent.

Ranking normally does not decrease.

Temporary poor performance should not erase years of growth.

# Design History

Originally,

Ranking was considered a higher level of Performance.

Later,

Odyssey separated the two concepts.

Performance became today's contribution.

Ranking became lifetime growth.

This separation became one of Odyssey's core philosophies.
