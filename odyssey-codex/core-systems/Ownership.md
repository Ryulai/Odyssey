# Ownership System

## Definition

The Ownership System develops members from contributors into owners.

Ownership is not about equity or job titles.

It represents the willingness and capability to take responsibility for the long-term success of Odyssey.

Ownership is the highest stage of personal and organizational maturity.

---

# Purpose

The Ownership System exists to:

- Develop future leaders.
- Encourage long-term thinking.
- Increase organizational responsibility.
- Build self-managing teams.
- Prepare future partners and shareholders.

Ownership transforms employees into builders.

---

# Why

Most organizations evaluate only execution.

Odyssey believes long-term organizations are built by people who think like owners.

Ownership creates leaders who protect the organization instead of only completing assigned work.

---

# What is NOT

The Ownership System is NOT:

- Company shares
- Job position
- Seniority
- Salary level
- Management authority

Ownership is a mindset supported by measurable behaviour.

# Design Philosophy

Odyssey believes ownership is earned.

People should first prove they can manage themselves.

Then they prove they can help others.

Finally they prove they can protect and grow the organization.

Ownership is the natural destination of long-term growth.

# Design History

Ownership was originally considered the final promotion stage.

During discussion,

it evolved into an independent Core System because ownership requires different behaviours from performance or mentorship.

The philosophy has been established.

The implementation has not yet been designed.
