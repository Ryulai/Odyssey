# Mentorship System

## Definition

The Mentorship System measures a member's ability to develop other people.

Unlike the Performance System, which evaluates personal contribution, the Mentorship System evaluates the ability to multiply the growth of others.

A mentor succeeds when the people around them become stronger.

---

# Purpose

The Mentorship System exists to:

- Develop future leaders.
- Build a sustainable learning culture.
- Encourage knowledge sharing.
- Multiply organizational capability.

Great organizations grow by developing people, not by depending on individuals.

---

# Why

A high performer is not necessarily a good mentor.

Odyssey distinguishes personal excellence from leadership excellence.

Leadership begins when a member consistently helps others grow.

---

# What is NOT

The Mentorship System is NOT:

- Management position
- Job title
- Authority
- Seniority
- Performance score

Mentorship evaluates influence through development.
