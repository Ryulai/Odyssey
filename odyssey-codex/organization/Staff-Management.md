# Staff Management

## Definition

Staff Management defines how members are onboarded, assigned, moved and offboarded inside the guild.

It is the operational layer that connects a person to their Identities, Fleet and Reviews.

---

# Purpose

- Keep member records accurate and auditable
- Give Captains and Directors clear operating tools
- Preserve every historical assignment for future reference
