# Fleet System

## Definition

The Fleet System organizes physical locations, teams and their operational identity inside Odyssey.

A Fleet is the operational unit — a shop, a branch, a squad — under a single Captain.

---

# Purpose

- Give every location a clear identity
- Provide a structure for staff assignment
- Establish accountability at the operational level
