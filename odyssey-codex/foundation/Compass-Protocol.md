# Compass Protocol

## Definition

Compass Protocol is the mandatory design validation process of Odyssey.

Every official concept, framework, system, project or language must complete the Compass Protocol before entering development or Freeze.

Compass Protocol ensures every design has purpose before execution.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Compass Protocol is to prevent meaningless, duplicated or contradictory designs.

It ensures every component inside Odyssey exists for a clear reason.

---

# Why

Many systems fail because people immediately discuss implementation before understanding the purpose.

Odyssey works in the opposite direction.

Purpose always comes before execution.

Understanding always comes before development.

---

# The Seven Questions

Every proposal must answer the following questions.

## Why

Why should this exist?

Why is it necessary?

---

## What

What exactly is it?

---

## What is NOT

What should people never misunderstand it as?

What are its boundaries?

---

## Who

Who is the target user?

Who will use it?

Who will maintain it?

---

## When

When should it be used?

When should it not be used?

---

## How

How will it work?

How will it be implemented?

---

## Future

How can it evolve?

How can it scale in the future?

# Current Status

Frozen.

Compass Protocol is mandatory.

No official design may bypass this process.

---

# Exceptions

Small operational adjustments do not require a complete Compass Protocol.

However,

any new Concept, Framework, Language, Rule or System must complete it.

---

# Future

Compass Protocol may expand with additional design questions.

However,

the seven core questions remain the minimum standard.

---

# Recovery Note

If rebuilding Odyssey,

Compass Protocol should always be restored before designing any new system.

It is the gateway of every official design.
