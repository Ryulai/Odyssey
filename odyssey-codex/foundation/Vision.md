# Vision

## Definition

The long-term aspiration of Odyssey — the world we intend to create.

The Vision describes the destination.
Every system, every decision, every review must point back to it.

---

# Purpose

The Vision exists to align every member across every generation.

A shared destination transforms individual effort into collective momentum.
