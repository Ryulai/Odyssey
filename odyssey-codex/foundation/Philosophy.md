# Philosophy

## Definition

Odyssey is inspired by Role Playing Games (RPG), but it is not a game.

It is a real-world growth framework that transforms invisible personal and organizational growth into a visible, understandable and measurable journey.

Every individual has an identity, a role, a purpose, and a path of progression. Odyssey provides the world, language, systems and rules that allow this journey to be understood, measured and continuously improved.

---

# Why

Traditional organizations evaluate only results.

Odyssey believes that growth is much larger than results.

Growth includes:

- Identity
- Behaviour
- Contribution
- Leadership
- Mentorship
- Responsibility
- Ownership

These elements are often invisible.

Odyssey exists to make invisible growth visible.

---

# Core Belief

Life is a journey.

Growth is a choice.

Everyone has a role.

Every role can evolve.

Every decision shapes the future.

---

# RPG Inspiration

Odyssey borrows the structure of RPGs because RPGs are one of the clearest examples of human progression.

Players naturally understand concepts such as:

- Level
- Class
- Rank
- Experience
- Achievement
- Mentorship
- Team
- Guild
- Ownership

Odyssey applies these ideas to real life.

However,

Odyssey is NOT a game.

It is a real organizational operating system inspired by RPG principles.

---

# World View

Odyssey is treated as its own civilization.

It has its own:

- Language
- Constitution
- Philosophy
- Systems
- Frameworks
- Rules
- Progression
- Values

Just as every civilization has its own culture,

Odyssey has its own culture.

---

# Principles

Every system should answer:

- Why does it exist?
- What problem does it solve?
- What should happen?
- What should NOT happen?

Every design must be intentional.

Nothing exists without purpose.
