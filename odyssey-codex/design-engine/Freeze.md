# Freeze

## Definition

Freeze is the official confirmation process of Odyssey.

When a concept enters Freeze, it becomes the current official version and may be used for development.

Freeze does not mean the concept can never change.

It means the current design is considered stable enough to move forward.

---

# Purpose

Freeze exists to prevent continuous redesign.

Without Freeze,

projects remain in endless discussion and development cannot begin.

Freeze creates a stable checkpoint.

---

# Why

Odyssey is expected to evolve for many years.

If every discussion continuously changes previous work,

the system will never become stable.

Freeze allows Odyssey to progress while preserving knowledge.

---

# What is NOT

Freeze is NOT:

- Permanent
- Perfect
- Final
- Unchangeable

Freeze is only the current official version.

Future versions may replace it.

---

# Freeze Conditions

A concept should only enter Freeze when:

- The objective is clear.
- The definition is complete.
- Compass Protocol has been completed.
- The Framework is stable.
- Major logical conflicts have been resolved.
- Future expansion has been considered.

---

# Freeze Levels

## 🧊 Frozen

Official.

Ready for implementation.

---

## 🟢 Discussed

Discussion completed.

Waiting for validation.

---

## 🟡 In Progress

Still under active design.

---

## 💡 Concept

Idea exists.

No detailed design yet.

---

## 🅿 Parking Slot

Future work.

Temporarily postponed.

---

## ❌ Rejected

The proposal has been intentionally abandoned.

The rejection reason must always be preserved.

---

# Unfreeze

A Frozen concept may be reopened only when:

- A major contradiction is discovered.
- A better architecture is found.
- Multiple systems are affected.
- The original assumption is no longer valid.

Small improvements should not trigger Unfreeze.
