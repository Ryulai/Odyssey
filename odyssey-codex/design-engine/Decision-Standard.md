# Decision Standard

## Definition

Decision Standard defines how official decisions are made inside Odyssey.

It ensures every important decision is logical, traceable and understandable by future generations.

A decision is part of the knowledge of Odyssey.

It should never depend solely on memory.

---

# Purpose

Decision Standard preserves the reasoning behind every important design.

It prevents repeated discussions of the same problem.

It allows future designers to understand why a decision was made.

---

# Why

A conclusion without reasoning eventually loses its value.

When people understand only the result,

they often redesign the same problem.

Odyssey preserves both the conclusion and the reasoning.

---

# Decision Requirements

Every important decision should include:

- Problem
- Context
- Alternatives
- Decision
- Reason
- Expected Impact
- Future Review Condition

# Decision Principles

- Every major decision must be documented.
- Every rejected proposal should preserve its reason.
- Every accepted proposal should explain why it was selected.
- Decisions should be objective instead of emotional.
- Decisions should align with the Constitution.
