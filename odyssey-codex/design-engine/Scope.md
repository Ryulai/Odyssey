# Scope

## Definition

Scope is the completeness checklist of Odyssey.

It does not decide whether an idea is good or bad.

Instead, it verifies whether a design has been considered from every important perspective before entering Freeze.

Scope measures completeness, not quality.

---

# Purpose

A good idea is not necessarily a complete idea.

Scope exists to prevent incomplete systems from entering development.

It provides designers with a structured checklist before implementation.

---

# Why

As Odyssey grows,

systems become increasingly interconnected.

Missing a single design aspect may affect multiple systems.

Scope ensures every important area has been reviewed.

---

# What is NOT

Scope is NOT:

- A Framework
- A Formula
- A KPI
- A Workflow
- A Development Checklist

Scope is only a Design Completeness Checklist.
