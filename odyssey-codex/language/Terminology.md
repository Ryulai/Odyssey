# Terminology

## Definition

Terminology is the official dictionary of Odyssey.

Every official keyword should be recorded here.

This document acts as the single source of truth for all terminology used throughout the Odyssey ecosystem.

---

# Purpose

Terminology ensures every official word has only one meaning.

It prevents inconsistent naming and misunderstanding.

Every official keyword should first appear here before being widely adopted.

---

# Current Official Terminology

## Achievement

A long-term collection of accomplishments.

Achievements reward extra contribution outside the normal Performance System.

Achievements do not directly affect Ranking.

---

## Anchor

The collective name for repeated behaviours that slow personal, team or organizational growth.

Anchor evaluates behaviour rather than personality.

---

## Class

A member's professional identity within Odyssey.

Each member owns one Main Class and may unlock additional Secondary Classes.

---

## Compass Protocol

The official seven-question validation process used before designing any system or concept.

---

## Constitution

The highest governing principles of Odyssey.

Every system must comply with the Constitution.

---

## Definition

The official explanation that defines the identity, purpose and boundaries of a concept.

---

## Framework

The structural blueprint of a system.

A Framework defines structure, not calculation.

---

## Freeze

The official confirmation of the current stable version.

Freeze represents a milestone, not a permanent conclusion.

---

## Main Class

The primary profession of an Odyssey member.

---

## Odyssey

The organizational world built around its own philosophy, language, systems and civilization.

Inspired by RPG design principles.

Not a game.

---

## Parking Slot

Ideas intentionally postponed for future discussion.

Parking Slot prevents valuable ideas from being forgotten.

---

## Performance

Current contribution measured within a specific period.

Performance reflects today's contribution.

---

## Ranking

Permanent growth accumulated through consistent long-term performance.

Ranking represents trust and maturity.

---

## Scope

The completeness checklist used before Freeze.

Scope measures completeness, not quality.

---

## Secondary Class

An additional profession unlocked after reaching the required Main Class Rank.

Each Secondary Class owns an independent Performance and Ranking system.

# Reserved Terms

- Anchor
- Constitution
- Compass Protocol
- Framework
- Freeze
- Scope
- Performance
- Ranking
- Achievement
- Main Class
- Secondary Class

---

# Naming Rules

Every new keyword should:

- Have one official definition.
- Represent only one concept.
- Avoid duplication.
- Follow the philosophy of Odyssey.
- Be added to this Terminology before official adoption.

---

# Design History

Terminology was originally treated as a glossary.

During development, it became clear that terminology is one of the foundations of Odyssey culture.

It is now maintained as a living dictionary.
