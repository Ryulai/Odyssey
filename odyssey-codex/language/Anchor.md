# Anchor

## Definition

Anchor is the collective term for behaviours that prevent an individual, team or organization from moving forward.

Anchor is the greatest internal enemy of Odyssey.

It does not describe personality.

It does not judge emotion.

It identifies repeated behaviours that continuously slow down growth, damage teamwork or prevent progress.

---

# Purpose

Anchor exists to create a common language for identifying growth obstacles.

Instead of criticizing people,

Odyssey discusses behaviours.

The objective is improvement,

not punishment.

---

# Why

Every organization has invisible behaviours that reduce performance.

Most organizations describe these behaviours differently.

Odyssey groups them under one shared language:

Anchor.

This creates faster communication and consistent understanding.

---

# What Anchor is NOT

Anchor is NOT:

- A personality type
- A mental health diagnosis
- An emotion
- A performance score
- A ranking deduction

Anchor evaluates repeated behaviour,

not personal identity.

---

# Core Principle

Emotion is natural.

Behaviour is a choice.

Odyssey never judges emotions.

Evaluation begins only when emotions repeatedly influence behaviour, judgement, teamwork or organizational progress.

---

# Current Design

Anchor is a major cultural concept.

It is positioned as the opposite force of growth.

Growth moves people forward.

Anchor holds people back.

---

# Current Behaviour Categories

The following categories are currently under discussion.

## Responsibility

Repeated avoidance of responsibility.

Habitual excuse making.

Lack of ownership.

---

## Integrity

Dishonesty.

Breaking trust.

Hiding mistakes.

Manipulating facts.

---

## Learning

Rejecting improvement.

Refusing feedback.

Believing there is nothing left to learn.

---

## Teamwork

Ignoring the team.

Damaging collaboration.

Creating unnecessary conflict.

Prioritising self over the organization.

---

## Emotion

Repeated emotional behaviour that affects judgement, professionalism or teamwork.

Emotion itself is not evaluated.

Behaviour caused by uncontrolled emotion is.

---

## Respect

Disrespect towards people, systems or organizational culture.

Ignoring agreed standards.

---

## Envy

Allowing comparison or jealousy to influence behaviour, teamwork or decision making.

---

# Current Discussion

The original inspiration came from the Seven Deadly Sins.

After discussion,

Odyssey decided NOT to directly adopt that concept.

Instead,

it uses the same idea as inspiration while creating its own behavioural language.

This better reflects Odyssey's identity and avoids unnecessary religious or cultural association.

# Influence

Anchor does NOT directly reduce:

- Performance Score
- Ranking
- Achievement

However,

serious or repeated Anchor behaviour may suspend:

- Promotion
- Bonus eligibility
- Additional rewards
- Leadership opportunities

until sufficient improvement has been demonstrated.

---

# Design History

Originally,

Anchor was discussed as a simple negative status.

Later,

it evolved into the central language describing behavioural obstacles.

It became one of the core concepts of Odyssey culture.
