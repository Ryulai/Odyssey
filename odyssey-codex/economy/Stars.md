# Stars

## Definition

Stars are a proposed secondary currency inside Odyssey — a scarce, prestige-oriented resource distinct from Legacy.

---

# Purpose

To reward exceptional moments that Legacy alone cannot fully honour — for example, once-a-year achievements or Director-granted recognitions.

---

# Current Status

🅿️ Parking Lot.

Approved future enhancement, intentionally postponed until the Legacy Economy is frozen.
The concept is reserved so that naming and mechanics do not drift.
