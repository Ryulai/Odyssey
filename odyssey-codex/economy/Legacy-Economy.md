# Legacy Economy

## Definition

The Legacy Economy is the internal reward economy of Odyssey.

Legacy is earned through contribution, growth and mentorship.
It is spent on cosmetics, collections, and long-term status.

---

# Purpose

- Convert intangible growth into a tangible reward
- Encourage long-term participation
- Fuel the Collection, Fleet and Identity systems
