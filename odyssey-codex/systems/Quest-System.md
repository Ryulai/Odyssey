# Quest System

## Definition

The Quest System will deliver short, structured missions that members can accept, complete and be rewarded for.

Quests turn daily work into an interactive, game-like experience.

---

# Purpose

- Make everyday contribution feel meaningful
- Provide a structured way to onboard new initiatives
- Reward focused, time-boxed effort
